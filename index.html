Our Services
Residential | Commercial | Industrial
  • Residential Solar Solutions
    Designed for personal living spaces to lower monthly household power bills and achieve home energy independence.
  • Where it is used: Independent houses, villas, apartment rooftops, residential societies, and farmhouses.
  • System Capacity: Typically 1 kW to 10 kW.
  • Key Focus: Household appliances (refrigerators, ACs, lights, water heaters), visual integration with home architecture, and maximizing government central subsidies (such as PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana up to 3 kW / ₹78,000).
  • Commercial Solar Solutions
    DDeployed by businesses and public institutions operating standard office hours to cut peak-hour operational expenses (OpEx).
  • Where it is used: Office buildings, retail malls, schools, universities, hospitals, hotels, software parks, and commercial carports.
  • System Capacity: Typically 10 kW to 100+ kW.
  • Key Focus: Reducing expensive daytime commercial tariff rates, optimizing corporate ESG (environmental/sustainability) goals, and utilizing Accelerated Depreciation (AD) tax benefits.
  • Industrial Solar Solutions
    Heavy-duty, high-capacity installations built to supply power to heavy machinery and continuous manufacturing operations.
  • Where it is used: Manufacturing plants, factories, warehouses, cold storage units, textile mills, auto plants, and industrial parks.
  • System Capacity: 100 kW to Multi-Megawatt (MW) scale.
  • Key Focus: Handling massive daytime power loads, lowering unit production costs, protecting against power surges with microgrids/energy storage (BESS), and structural/ground-mount flexibility.

Difference between DCR and NON DCR
Feature DCR Solar Panels Non-DCR Solar Panels
Manufacturing Origin 100% Made in India (Both solar cells and solar modules must be manufactured domestically). Imported or Mixed (Solar cells are usually imported from countries like China/Vietnam, though assembly may happen in India).
Government Subsidy Eligibility Eligible for PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana and government tenders. Not Eligible for government subsidies or restricted tenders.
Target Audience Residential homeowners applying for subsidies, government facilities, and PSU projects. Commercial, industrial, and private utility-scale solar projects.
Price Point Slightly higher cost due to domestic manufacturing standards and compliance. Lower initial cost due to cheaper imported raw materials/cells.
Quality & Warranty High quality, backed by strict Indian manufacturing compliance (ALMM/BIS approved). Quality varies depending on the source country and manufacturer standards.

Difference between On-Grid and Off Grid and Hybrid solar
Feature On-Grid Solar System Off-Grid Solar System Hybrid Solar System
Grid Connection Connected to local power grid Completely disconnected from grid Connected to local power grid
Battery Storage No battery bank Uses a battery bank Uses a battery bank
Power Outage Protection Shuts down (no power backup) Stays on (uses battery power) Stays on (uses battery power)
Excess Energy Handling Exports to grid via Net Metering Lost or curtailed once battery is full Charges battery first, exports rest to grid
Primary Goal Maximize bill reduction & lowest cost Complete energy independence in remote areas Bill reduction + power backup during cuts
Upfront Cost Lowest Moderate Highest
